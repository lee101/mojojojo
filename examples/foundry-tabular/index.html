Tabular classifiers, served without pickle.

Five research baselines (adult, credit-g, bank-marketing, phoneme, diabetes) as flat tree and linear arrays. Probabilities match the scikit-learn originals to 1e-7. Not for credit, hiring, insurance or medical decisions.

Calls require your mojojojo API key and use your compute credits. Call from your own terminal.
