Compound Growth

The finance calculator that lives in every group chat. Monthly compounding, annual raises, inflation — computed by kernels that compile to native Mojo via mojosub and are verified against CPython.

The kernels (self-contained)

def project_final(principal, monthly, annual_return, years, raise):
    balance = principal
    r = (1 + annual_return) ** (1/12) - 1
    c = monthly
    for y in range(years):
        for m in range(12):
            balance = balance * (1 + r) + c
        c *= (1 + raise)
    return balance

Three scalar loops, no lists. Strings and shapes stay in the handler; the math is what compiles. Same rule as Trendfolio.
