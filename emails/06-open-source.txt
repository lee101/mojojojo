The hosted service is one consumer of separate MIT-licensed projects: mojosub, the hybrid compiler, the mjj agent, and the factory that builds and tests library ports.

A generated repository is not automatically a ready port. The live service only marks ports ready after integration checks; each project owns its own tests and benchmark notes.
